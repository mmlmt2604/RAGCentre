# Workshop Procedures (staff only)

These procedures apply to every mechanic working in a Kestrel Cycles workshop.

## Booking in a bike

Record the frame serial number, the customer's phone number and the requested work in the job
system before touching the bike. Photograph both sides of the bike to document its condition.
Attach the numbered job tag to the saddle rail.

## Torque settings

Always use a calibrated torque wrench on carbon parts. Unless the part is marked otherwise, use
these values:

| Part | Torque |
|---|---|
| Stem bolts (handlebar clamp) | 5 Nm |
| Stem bolts (steerer clamp) | 6 Nm |
| Seatpost clamp, carbon post | 6 Nm |
| Brake caliper mounting bolts | 6 to 8 Nm |
| Pedals | 35 Nm |
| Cassette lockring | 40 Nm |

Apply carbon assembly paste to carbon clamping surfaces. Never grease a carbon steerer or seatpost.

## Free six-week service

The free first service covers gear and brake cable tension, spoke tension check, bolt check with
the torque table above, and a safety test ride. Replacement parts are charged at the normal price.

## Warranty claims

Inspect the claimed part, photograph the defect next to the job tag, and file the claim in the
supplier portal within two working days. Do not repair a frame under warranty before the supplier
approves the claim, because any repair voids the supplier's warranty.

## Battery handling

Damaged or swollen e-bike batteries go straight into the fireproof battery cabinet, never into the
general parts store. Charge batteries only on the metal bench under the smoke detector, and never
leave a charging battery unattended at closing time.

## End of day

Clean and return all tools to the shadow boards. Sweep metal filings from the truing stands. Log
any tool that is missing or damaged in the workshop notebook so it can be replaced before the next
shift.
