# Kestrel Cycles Warranty

Every bicycle sold by Kestrel Cycles is covered by the warranty described here. The warranty
applies to the original owner only and starts on the date shown on the purchase receipt. Keep
the receipt: we ask for it with every claim.

## What is covered

### Frames and rigid forks

Aluminium and steel frames carry a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and
workmanship for the original owner. Carbon frames are covered for seven years. Rigid forks share
the warranty of the frame they were sold with.

### Suspension forks and shocks

Suspension forks and rear shocks are covered for two years. Seals, bushings and oil are wear
parts and are not covered after the first 90 days.

### Components

Drivetrain parts, brakes, wheels, cockpit parts and saddles are covered for two years against
manufacturing defects. Tyres, inner tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes and bar tape are wear
items and are covered only if they fail within 30 days of purchase.

### Electric bikes

Motors, batteries and displays on our e-bikes are covered for three years. The battery is
considered defective if it holds less than 60 percent of its rated capacity within the warranty
period, provided it was charged with the supplied charger.

## What is not covered

- Normal wear and tear, including worn tyres, chains and brake pads.
- Damage from crashes, impacts, misuse or improper assembly.
- Bikes used for rental, commercial delivery or competition.
- Paint chips and cosmetic marks that do not affect the frame's strength.
- Modifications such as fitting a motor kit to a non-electric frame, which voids the frame
  warranty.

## How to make a claim

Bring the bike and the receipt to any Kestrel Cycles store, or email claims@kestrel.example
with photos of the problem and the frame serial number. The serial number is stamped under the
bottom bracket. We aim to inspect claimed bikes within five working days. If a part is
defective we repair or replace it at no cost; when an identical part is no longer made we fit
one of equal or better specification.

## Crash replacement

If you damage a carbon frame in a crash within the first three years, you can buy a replacement
frame at 40 percent off the current list price. This crash replacement offer is separate from the
warranty and needs the damaged frame to be returned to us.
