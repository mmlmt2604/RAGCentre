# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times

Orders placed before 2 pm on a working day leave our warehouse the same day. Standard delivery
takes two to four working days within the country. Express delivery arrives the next working day
when ordered before noon.

## Delivery costs

| Service | Cost | Free above |
|---|---|---|
| Standard parcel | 4.95 euro | 60 euro |
| Express parcel | 9.95 euro | never |
| Bicycle box | 39 euro | 1500 euro |

## Bicycles

Bicycles ship 90 percent assembled in a reinforced box. You need to fit the handlebar, pedals and
front wheel, and set the saddle height. Every bike box includes a multi-tool and an illustrated
assembly sheet. If you would rather not assemble it yourself, choose click and collect: the bike
is built and safety-checked by a mechanic and waits for you at the store of your choice.

## International orders

We ship parts and clothing to all European Union countries. Bicycles and e-bike batteries ship
only within the country, because batteries are classed as dangerous goods for air and road
freight across borders.

## Damaged parcels

Check the box when the courier arrives. If it is visibly damaged, note this on the courier's
device before signing, then photograph the box and contact us within 48 hours. We send a
replacement as soon as the courier confirms the damage report.

## Tracking

You receive a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves the warehouse. Parcels that cannot be
delivered are held at the nearest courier point for seven days before being returned to us.
