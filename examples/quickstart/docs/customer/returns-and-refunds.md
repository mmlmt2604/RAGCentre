# Returns and Refunds

We want you to be happy with what you buy. This page explains how to return items to Kestrel
Cycles and how refunds are paid.

## Return window

Unused accessories, clothing and parts can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund. Bicycles can be returned within 14 days if they have been ridden less than 25
kilometres and show no signs of outdoor use. Items bought in the winter sale can be returned
until the end of January.

## Items we cannot take back

- Helmets, gloves and bib shorts once removed from their sealed packaging, for hygiene reasons.
- Custom-built wheels and bikes assembled to your measurements.
- Gift cards.
- Tyres that have been mounted on a rim.

## How to return an item

Start a return from the Orders page of your account and print the prepaid label. Pack the item in
its original box with all manuals and small parts. Drop the parcel at any partner courier point
and keep the receipt as proof of postage. Store returns are also welcome: bring the item and your
order number to the counter.

## Refunds

We refund to the original payment method within five working days of receiving the return. Card
refunds can take another three to five days to appear on your statement. Original delivery costs
are refunded only when the whole order is returned. Return postage is free for faulty or wrongly
sent items; otherwise a flat 6 euro fee is deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges

To swap a size or colour, return the original item and place a new order. This is the fastest way
to get the item you want, because the new order ships straight away instead of waiting for the
return to arrive.
